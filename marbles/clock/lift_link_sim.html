F, the spine with the entry and exit flanges, hangs from the pivot (its SK8, off-centre on its side) and only rocks. M, the sliding frame with a sieve on each side, slides along F. A rigid link from the fixed frame holds an 8 mm axle clamped in an SK8 on M's double keeper, on M's side away from F's flanges, so rocking slides M against F: that slide is the lift.
